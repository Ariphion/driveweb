Our Mission & Expertise

At GammaTech, we bridge the gap between complex technical errors and seamless, peak-performance computing environments.

Our foundational core mission is to provide uncompromising, elite-tier restoration and system tuning services for consumer and enterprise digital infrastructure. We operate under the philosophy that technology should always serve as an accelerator rather than a frustration. Through methodical multi-point diagnostic assessments, precision hardware re-engineering, and tailored software optimizations, we guarantee your systems run cooler, faster, and significantly more reliably over extended lifecycles.

Who We Are

Numbered among the dedicated tech pioneers in the region, GammaTech was established in Tbilisi to provide rapid, transparent, and ultra-precise computer diagnostics, component-level repairs, and software optimization services. What began as an agile group of component-level hardware engineers has matured into a sophisticated, state-of-the-art repair and recovery hub trusted by everyday consumers, competitive e-sports athletes, and corporate enterprise operations alike. We treat every desktop workstation, high-end gaming machine, and corporate server with specialized, professional care.

Our workflow structure stands apart by rejecting temporary, surface-level fixes or superficial registry patches. When a computer configuration enters our laboratory environment, it goes through rigorous baseline evaluations to expose underlying thermal bottlenecks, hardware degradation points, and micro-voltage inconsistencies. Our engineering staff undergoes perpetual technical development, mastering the latest methodologies in micro-soldering, data carving, and software virtualization frameworks to remain continuously ahead of technological shifts.

Transparency forms the backbone of our community integration. We generate clear, structured, and itemized diagnostics readouts so that our clients understand precisely what their equipment demands to achieve sustainable, factory-peak optimization levels without unexpected overhead costs.

Inside Our Repair Laboratory

Component Diagnostics Bench

Our lab is equipped with advanced diagnostic oscilloscopes and precise micro-soldering stations to detect and resolve intricate motherboard and hardware-level failures.

By tracing intricate logic board schematics, diagnosing voltage rails, and conducting precise hot-air reflow adjustments, our hardware specialists successfully salvage high-value logic boards, graphics controllers, and power modules that typical service centers prematurely label unfixable.

OS & Software Optimization Hub

An isolated network environment dedicated exclusively to clean operating system re-installations, complex malware eradication, and file registry performance modifications.

We deeply analyze active background daemons, driver conflicts, and core kernel configurations to remove operational latency entirely. Our aggressive profiling methods strip out system bloatware, adjust thread priorities, and streamline resource pooling frameworks to deliver lightning-fast boot sequences and maximum frame rates.

Secure Data Recovery Zone

A specialized, static-free workspace utilized to securely disassemble storage drives, bypass physical component failures, and extract critical user backup files safely.

Leveraging deep-level raw sector imaging hardware and firmware modification utilities, our recovery team safely bypasses mechanical read-write head degradation and controller board corruption. We prioritize absolute data privacy while successfully reclaiming critical business documents, personal media archives, and configuration backups from structurally compromised storage systems.
